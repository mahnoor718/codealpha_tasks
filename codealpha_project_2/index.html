Phishing Awareness Center

Learn about common cyber threats, social engineering, and safe online practices.

🎣

Phishing

Fraudulent messages designed to trick people into sharing sensitive information or interacting with unsafe links.

🎯

Spear Phishing

A targeted attack using personal or organizational information to make a deceptive message appear trustworthy.

📱

Smishing

Phishing attempts delivered through SMS or messaging applications.

☎️

Vishing

Voice-based social engineering involving impersonation of trusted organizations or representatives.

🧠

Social Engineering

Manipulating emotions such as fear, urgency, curiosity, or trust to influence decisions.

🌐

Fake Websites

Lookalike websites designed to imitate legitimate services and collect sensitive information.
